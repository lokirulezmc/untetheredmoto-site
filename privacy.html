Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 6, 2026  ·  Last updated: June 8, 2026

Short version: We collect only what the app needs to work. We don't sell your data, run ads, or require an account. Your emergency contact and saved places never leave your device.
Section 01

Data We Collect

Location (GPS)

WhatPrecise GPS coordinates and speed while the app is in use. When Riding Mode is active, location is collected in the background via a foreground GPS service.
WhyTo show your position on the map, calculate speed for Riding Mode and Auto DND, provide turn-by-turn navigation, and attach a coordinate to hazard reports you submit.
StoredProcessed on-device in real time. Not stored on our servers. Hazard reports include a single coordinate with no personally identifying information.

Contacts (Read-Only)

WhatName and phone number of one contact you select as your emergency contact.
WhyThe Auto SOS feature sends an emergency SMS with your GPS location if a crash is detected and you do not cancel the alert.
StoredStored locally on your device in encrypted storage (Android Keystore). Never transmitted to our servers or any third party.

Saved Places (Quick Nav)

WhatLabels and GPS coordinates for saved locations (Home, Work, Favorites).
WhyOne-tap navigation to frequently visited destinations.
StoredStored locally on your device in encrypted storage. Never transmitted externally.

Crash and Diagnostic Data

WhatApp crash logs including error message, stack trace, device model, Android version, and app version at time of crash.
WhyTo identify and fix bugs.
StoredJavaScript-layer crashes are sent to our own self-hosted server. Native (system-level) crashes are reported to Firebase Crashlytics (Google). Neither system includes location data or contact information.

Purchase and Subscription Data

WhatSubscription status (free or Pro), purchase date, and an anonymous customer ID.
WhyTo unlock Pro features when you have an active subscription.
StoredManaged by RevenueCat. Untethered Moto does not store payment card information.

Hazard Reports

WhatHazard type (e.g., pothole, gravel), GPS coordinate, and an anonymous device ID generated on first launch.
WhyTo add community-sourced road warnings to the map for other riders.
StoredStored on our self-hosted server. Auto-expires after 24 hours. Device ID is random and not linked to your identity.
Section 02

Data We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect your name, email address, or any account credentials
  • We do not record or store your ride routes or trip history on our servers
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your data with advertisers or data brokers
  • There are no ads in this app
  • We do not collect data from your contacts list beyond the single number you manually select
Section 03

Analytics

Untethered Moto plans to use PostHog for privacy-first, self-hosted analytics (feature usage counts, no user tracking). Analytics are not yet active. This policy will be updated when they go live. No personally identifying information will ever be included.

Section 05

Background Location

Untethered Moto requests background location permission to support Riding Mode. When active, the app continues tracking your GPS while the screen is off or the app is in the background. This is used solely to:

  • Keep navigation active and advance turn-by-turn guidance
  • Maintain the foreground GPS service that powers speed-based Auto DND
  • Trigger the crash detection sensor

Background location is only active when the app's foreground service is running. You can stop it at any time by stopping navigation or closing the app.

Section 06

Data Retention

  • On-device data (emergency contact, saved places, settings) - retained until you delete it in the app or uninstall
  • Hazard reports - deleted automatically after 24 hours, or sooner if enough riders report it as cleared
  • JS crash reports - stored on our self-hosted server, retained until reviewed and resolved
  • Native crash logs - retained by Firebase Crashlytics per their retention policy (90 days)
  • Subscription records - retained by RevenueCat per their retention policy
Section 07

Children

Untethered Moto is intended for licensed motorcycle riders. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

Section 08

Your Rights

  • Delete your emergency contact and saved places at any time from within the app (Riding Preferences and Settings)
  • Request deletion of any hazard reports associated with your anonymous device ID by contacting us
  • Opt out of native crash reporting by disabling Firebase analytics on your Android device (Settings > Google > Ads). JavaScript crash reports cannot be disabled as they are required for app stability monitoring.
Section 09

Changes to This Policy

We will update this page when our data practices change. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects when changes were made.
