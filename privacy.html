Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 6, 2026  ·  Last updated: June 6, 2026

Untethered Moto is a motorcycle riding companion app for Android. This policy explains what data the app collects, why, and how it is handled.

Short version: we collect only what the app needs to work. We don't sell your data, run ads, or require an account.

1. Data We Collect

Location (GPS)

What: Precise GPS coordinates and speed while the app is in use. When Riding Mode is active, location is collected in the background via a foreground GPS service.

Why: To show your position on the map, calculate speed for Riding Mode and Auto Do Not Disturb, provide turn-by-turn navigation, and attach a coordinate to hazard reports you submit.

Stored: Location data is processed on-device in real time. It is not stored on our servers. Hazard reports include a single coordinate (where you reported the hazard) but contain no personally identifying information.

Contacts (Read-Only)

What: Name and phone number of one contact you select as your emergency contact.

Why: The Auto SOS feature sends an emergency SMS with your GPS location if a crash is detected and you do not cancel the alert.

Stored: Stored locally on your device in encrypted storage (Android Keystore). Never transmitted to our servers or any third party.

Saved Places (Quick Nav)

What: Labels and GPS coordinates for saved locations (Home, Work, Favorites).

Why: One-tap navigation to frequently visited destinations.

Stored: Stored locally on your device in encrypted storage. Never transmitted externally.

Crash and Diagnostic Data

What: App crash logs, device model, Android version, and app version at time of crash.

Why: To identify and fix bugs.

Stored: Sent to Firebase Crashlytics (Google). No location data or contact information is included in crash reports.

Purchase and Subscription Data

What: Subscription status (free or Pro), purchase date, and an anonymous customer ID.

Why: To unlock Pro features when you have an active subscription.

Stored: Managed by RevenueCat. Untethered Moto does not store payment card information.

Hazard Reports

What: Hazard type (e.g., pothole, gravel), GPS coordinate of the hazard, and an anonymous device ID generated on first launch.

Why: To add community-sourced road warnings to the map for other riders.

Stored: Stored on our self-hosted server. Reports expire automatically after 24 hours. The device ID is random and not linked to your identity or any other data.

2. Data We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect your name, email address, or any account credentials.
  • We do not record or store your ride routes or trip history on our servers.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your data with advertisers or data brokers.
  • There are no ads in this app.
  • We do not collect data from your contacts list beyond the single number you manually select.

3. Analytics

Untethered Moto plans to use PostHog for privacy-first, self-hosted analytics (feature usage counts, no user tracking). Analytics are not yet active. This policy will be updated when they go live. No personally identifying information will be included.

All map tiles, routing, geocoding, and points of interest are served from our own self-hosted infrastructure. These requests include your GPS coordinates to return relevant results but are not logged beyond standard server access logs (IP address, timestamp, request path).

5. Background Location

Untethered Moto requests background location permission to support Riding Mode. When active, the app continues tracking your GPS while the screen is off or the app is in the background. This is used solely to:

  • Keep navigation active and advance turn-by-turn guidance
  • Maintain the foreground GPS service that powers speed-based Auto DND
  • Trigger the crash detection sensor

Background location is only active when the app's foreground service is running. You can stop it at any time by stopping navigation or closing the app.

6. Data Retention

  • On-device data (emergency contact, saved places, app settings): retained until you delete it in the app or uninstall.
  • Hazard reports: deleted automatically after 24 hours, or sooner if enough riders report it as cleared.
  • Crash logs: retained by Firebase Crashlytics per their retention policy (90 days).
  • Subscription records: retained by RevenueCat per their retention policy.

7. Children

Untethered Moto is intended for licensed motorcycle riders. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

8. Your Rights

You may:

  • Delete your emergency contact and saved places at any time from within the app (Riding Preferences and Settings).
  • Request deletion of any hazard reports associated with your anonymous device ID by contacting us.
  • Opt out of crash reporting by disabling Firebase analytics on your Android device (Settings > Google > Ads).

9. Changes to This Policy

We will update this page when our data practices change. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects when changes were made.
