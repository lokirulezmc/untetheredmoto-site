Features

Everything a rider needs.
Nothing they don't.

Built from scratch for motorcycles. Not a car app with a different icon.

🗺️
Curvy Route Generator
Generate scenic loops from wherever you are. Twisties, not highways. Set your distance and let the app find the ride.
⛽
One-Tap Gas
Nearest station, no menus, no confirmation. One tap while riding and you're routed. Simple as it should be.
⚠️
Hazard Reports
Community-sourced road warnings in real time. Potholes, gravel, debris, police, road closures and more. Auto-expire after 24 hours.
🆘
Crash Detection + SOS
Detects a crash and automatically texts your emergency contact with your exact GPS location. Cancel if you're fine. Send if you're not.
🌤️
Ride Quality Score
Real-time weather conditions scored for riding. Rain, wind, fog, temperature - all factored in so you know before you go.
🏍️
Garage
Bike profiles, fuel log, and maintenance tracking. Know your MPG, track your service history, manage multiple bikes.
🎵
Spotify Integration
Full Spotify controls without leaving the app. Play, pause, skip, and browse your library - glove-safe large touch targets.
🚗
Android Auto
Full Android Auto support. Navigation, hazards, POI search and loop generation all accessible from your head unit.
🔕
Auto Do Not Disturb
Speed-triggered DND silences notifications when you're moving. Unlocks automatically when you stop. Fully configurable threshold.
